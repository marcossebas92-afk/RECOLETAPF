About Us

Who We Are

We are a group of parents from the SS.CC Recoleta School, who enjoy playing and competing in soccer in the evenings.

Our community has been growing since 2015, and today we have teams competing in three categories (Open, M40, and M48). As parents, we strive to promote healthy sportsmanship, good camaraderie, and a balanced lifestyle.

Every year we are proud to represent the school and strive for excellence in every competition, so that we can continue to be a great example for our children.

What We Believe In

For us Christ is the most important part of our identity, and he help us to overcome every challenge.

Faith goes hand in hand with Christ, so in every difficult moment, we have faith that all things are possible.

Commitment is the most important value, because by regarding ourselves as a family, every good deed is for us and on behalf of the team.

We always try to foster friendship, because this is a team sport, and friendship creates a more pleasant atmosphere.

Discipline helps us not only on the field but also in our activities at work and at home.

Mutual respect is the most important thing, as great things can be achieved in a healthy environment, and it all begins with respect.

Our Main Objective


Promoting friendship, teamwork, healthy lifestyle and competing through Recoleta's values.
