Welcome to Recoleta Soccer Parents Team

More than a Soccer team, we are a community of parents united by soccer, teamwork, and friendship. Here we are, so proud to represent SS.CC Recoleta in ADEPAFA competitions.

What Makes Us Special

Teamwork

We support each other every step of the way, both on and off the court.

Healthy Lifestyle

Fun

Every training session is always filled with laughter, jokes, and great experiences.

Promoting friendship, teamwork, healthy lifestyle and competing through Recoleta's values.
